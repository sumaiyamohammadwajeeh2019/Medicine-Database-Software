🕌 Halal Guide - Dr. Sumaiya

✅ HALAL: Tablets - Paracetamol, Metformin, Amlodipine - Synthetic
⚠️ CHECK: Capsule - Look HPMC not Gelatin, Syrup - Alcohol Free
❌ HARAM: Heparin porcine, Alcohol syrup >5%, Porcine gelatin
💡 TIP: Ask "HPMC cap? Alcohol free?" Tablet is most Halal
Emergency: Haram allowed if no alternative - Necessity principle
